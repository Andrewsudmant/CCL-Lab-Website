# ADR 0005: Reader-value theme copy and public research ideas

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-08-27
- Decision owner: Project owner

## Context

Gate 5D established a sound separation among themes, Work, publications and research ideas, but theme descriptions and idea cards could still read as well-organised topic lists. Topic coverage alone does not tell a reader what is unstable in current understanding, why the instability matters, or how research could change the answer. Evidence-gap language is particularly risky because absence alone does not establish research value.

## Decision

Each theme will communicate a problem of understanding through a stable understanding, an instability, a consequence, an analytical intervention, a changed understanding and a boundary. A restrained `What this changes` statement will make the conceptual payoff explicit.

Each research idea will contain a working title, question, problem of understanding, consequence, possible research design and methods connected to that design. The 24 owner-approved ideas will remain public because they clarify the lab's intellectual possibilities, but their item-level disclaimer and surrounding copy will state that they are not active or funded projects.

Evidence absence will not be treated as sufficient evidence of value. The value of additional evidence depends on whether it could change understanding, appraisal or action for a defined decision or community.

The Gate 5D previous-work examples are frozen during this gate. This prevents a theme-copy and idea-portfolio change from silently altering source-supported evidence relationships that require a separate owner review.

Current Conversations remains visible in navigation because it is part of the planned public architecture, but it remains explicitly in development. Fixture cards, detail pages and feeds are removed from the public build until a later authorised launch gate.

## Consequences

Theme and idea schemas now require the reader-value fields. Public theme pages are longer but use a consistent two-column/one-column card layout. Tests enforce 24 ideas, exact qualifications, the previous-work freeze, and the absence of Current Conversations fixtures from public output. The site carries a configuration-driven Draft 0.1 notice while deployment remains separately controlled.

## Alternatives considered

- Retaining short topic summaries was rejected because it hid the analytical payoff.
- Ranking all evidence gaps was rejected because relevance is decision- and context-dependent.
- Treating ideas as planned Work was rejected because it could imply commitment or funding.
- Editing previous-work examples alongside theme copy was deferred to avoid conflating editorial and source-verification decisions.

## Revisit triggers

Revisit if owner review changes a theme argument, if an idea becomes genuinely active Work, if previous-work evidence is separately approved, or if Current Conversations receives explicit launch authorisation.
