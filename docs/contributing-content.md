# Contributing public research content

Start with `AGENTS.md`, `docs/content-governance.md`, `docs/research-content-model.md` and the reader-value editorial standard. Edit canonical YAML/configuration rather than generated QMD fragments.

For a theme, identify the stable understanding, the instability, why it matters, the analytical intervention, what changes for the reader and the boundary. Do not make novelty or absence the argument.

For a research idea, provide every schema field and connect the possible design to the stated problem. Do not add funders, partners, dates, deliverables or findings unless the record is being deliberately migrated into verified Work through a separately reviewed change.

Do not alter the selected Gate 5D previous-work examples until their separate owner review. The freeze test is deliberately strict.

Run `make validate`, `make test`, `make build` and `make check`. Normal builds are offline. Do not enable discovery, paid calls, staging writes, deployment or Pages as part of a content change.
