# Gate 5H decision: human cadence and public accessibility

Date: 28 August 2026  
Status: accepted for Draft 0.1 owner review

## Decision

Preserve the lab's academic judgement while removing institutional smoothness from public copy. The site will lead with concrete problems, people and decisions; vary sentence and page structures; explain specialist terms at first use; and keep the nearest material qualification beside each claim. Structured records, evidence boundaries and detailed provenance remain intact beneath the public presentation.

## Diagnosis

The Gate 5G site was intellectually coherent, but several patterns made its authorship feel templated: four adjacent negative propositions with the same rhythm; repeated abstract-noun lists; identical research-idea headings; one five-part structure imposed on every Work page; formal exposition before examples; and repeated governance qualifications on reader-facing pages. None was a factual defect. Together they made readers work too hard before meeting a recognisable city problem.

## Consequences

- The homepage receives one memorable proposition and shorter supporting copy while retaining the Gate 5F claim in the research programme.
- Each theme opens with an explicitly illustrative scenario before the full academic argument.
- Research ideas retain every approved source field but use a lighter public card.
- Work pages use structures suited to programmes, papers, completed projects and tools.
- Our Approach begins with the active-travel illustration; Current Conversations begins in ordinary language.
- A controlled terminology map and deterministic diagnostic make future editorial review repeatable. Diagnostics inform human judgement and cannot rewrite copy or act as a release score.

## Preserved decisions

The four theme titles, order, scope, boundaries and relationships; all 24 idea questions and tier assignments; seven Work records, statuses and claim boundaries; 46 verified publication records; selected previous-work IDs, order, evidence and theme relationships; Current Conversations' in-development state; and the guarded Draft 0.1 release boundary remain fixed.
