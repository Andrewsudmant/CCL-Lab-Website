# Gate 0 and Gate 1 scope

## In scope

- Repository conventions, governance, security guidance, architecture and a foundational ADR.
- A polished, responsive, accessible Quarto website with the required navigation.
- Versioned schemas and fixture records for core lab and Research Watch content.
- Research-scope configuration for the six initial themes.
- A fixture-only Research Watch prototype separating reviewed demonstrations from unreviewed candidates.
- Local validation, generated listings, tests, static accessibility checks, internal-link checks and CI.

## Explicitly out of scope

- External API, feed, scraping, browser-automation, model, embedding, or Bluesky calls.
- Live discovery, summarisation, classification, deduplication, alerts, newsletters, or scheduled ingestion.
- Automated promotion of candidates into reviewed selections.
- Databases, search indexes, vector stores, queues, server-side APIs, authentication, reviewer dashboards, or content-management systems.
- Collection of personal data, subscriber forms, analytics, cookies, advertising, or behavioural tracking.
- Final research claims, complete biographies, final project descriptions, a definitive publications list, or owner-approved representative examples.
- Use of the SFU logo or other institutional brand assets not supplied and approved by the owner.
- Production hosting, DNS, domain configuration, public deployment, environment secrets, or merging to `main`.
- Final legal, privacy, accessibility, records-management, or institutional communications approval.
