# Gate 4B–5A scope

## In scope

- Rename the active/public Research Watch system to **Current Conversations**, preserve historical evidence and maintain accessible former-route pages.
- Introduce source and conversation-cluster records, mixed-source fixtures, feeds, filters and topic pages.
- Reconcile a complete publication inventory while retaining a smaller selected view and explicit MDPI controls.
- Add fail-closed paid-search budgets, bounded provider diagnostics, atomic private staging and automation-branch safeguards.
- Produce a 25-entry owner calibration exercise, model benchmark, local QA evidence and three review ZIPs.

## Explicitly out of scope

- Merging to `main`, public deployment, DNS/hosting changes or production scheduling.
- Paid API calls without all owner-approved credentials and CAD controls.
- Unrestricted crawling, paywall bypass, raw provider/model response retention or social-network surveillance.
- Treating captured fixtures as live/current discovery or implying unreviewed material is endorsed.
- Final model/provider selection, subscriber services, analytics, authentication or user accounts.
- Automated correction/removal without an auditable policy and owner-approved incident process.
