# Gate 5C project mapping table

Decision basis: owner-approved Gate 5C brief, 26 August 2026. Each project remains one canonical YAML record. Themes are analytical relationships; geographies, methods, sectors and climate domains remain separate facets.

| Project | Relationship to lab | Primary theme | Secondary themes | Boundary applied |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Geography of urban climate evidence | Foundational prior work informing current research | Geographies of Climate Learning | Where New Evidence Matters | Existing evidence geography and possible relevance across places are primary. Gaps become Theme 2 only where their consequences for learning or decisions are established. |
| Data Methodologies for Climate Impact Assessment | Foundational prior work | Geographies of Climate Learning | Consequences for People and Places | Scaling and geographical representation affect what can be inferred across places and which local consequences can be represented. Data and modelling alone do not justify Theme 2. |
| Climate delivery modes | Current lab research | Modes of Climate Delivery | Geographies of Climate Learning | The programme concerns configurations of authority, finance, coordination, participation, capability, sequencing and maintenance across places. No unpublished IPCC material or endorsement claim is used. |
| CoBen | Current lab research | Consequences for People and Places | Where New Evidence Matters; Modes of Climate Delivery | Conditional scenarios represent benefits, burdens, costs, risks and distribution while exposing evidence limits and implementation conditions. They are not predictions. |
| Occupational transition requirements and occupation switching | Current lab research | Consequences for People and Places | Where New Evidence Matters; Modes of Climate Delivery | Theme 2 concerns consequential calibration and unresolved transition requirements; Theme 3 concerns institutions, training and sector arrangements. No individual employment outcome is predicted. |
| UK Co-Benefits Atlas | Foundational prior work | Consequences for People and Places | Geographies of Climate Learning | The Atlas makes place-based benefits, costs and distribution visible. Tool status does not justify Theme 2, and policymaker relevance does not itself justify Theme 3. UK estimates are not universal. |

## Changes from thematic reframe v1

- Data Methodologies moved from Theme 2 to Theme 1 as primary; Theme 4 remains secondary.
- UK Co-Benefits Atlas removed Theme 2 and Theme 3 secondary assignments and added Theme 1.
- The other four owner-approved mappings were retained.
- No records were duplicated, and no facet was converted into a theme.
