## Governance checks

- All thirteen records validate against a schema separate from research work.
- Every record contains a question, a conservative one- or two-sentence reason it may matter, and at least one suggested method.
- Every record displays exactly: `Research idea · not currently an active or funded project`.
- All are `draft-owner-review`; none is described as approved, active, funded or underway.
- No idea has a work status, parent, funder, partner, date, deliverable, finding, recruitment field or publication ID.
- Generation includes ideas only in the distinct pale-ochre theme-page section. They are absent from Work, publication listings, Current Conversations, JSON/RSS feeds and active/funded counts.
- Method labels describe possible approaches, not methodological commitments. Settings are optional facets and do not imply selected study sites.

## Owner review questions

The owner should assess whether each question is intellectually useful, whether the one-sentence significance claim is appropriately cautious, whether the proposed methods are plausible, and whether any idea should be revised or withheld before public launch. Approval would change only `owner_review_status`; it would not turn an idea into active work.
