| `climate-delivery-modes` | Urban climate policies are often compared through their targets, technologies or formal instruments. That comparison can conceal the institutional arrangements that make the same intervention operable in one city and fragile in another. | How do configurations of authority, finance, organisational capability, participation, coordination, sequencing and maintenance shape whether urban climate action becomes possible and durable? | The programme develops and tests a comparative framework for distinguishing recurring delivery mechanisms from arrangements that remain dependent on particular city types, sectors or institutional conditions. | A clearer account of delivery modes could allow researchers and practitioners to compare climate action through the arrangements that make it work, rather than through policy labels alone. | Owner-approved current programme connected to a verified peer-reviewed framework paper. | No delivery configuration is presented as universally suitable or effective, and no unpublished IPCC material is used. | Owner-approved Gate 5D programme description; Nature publisher record for the delivery-modes framework (prospective) | none |
| `coben-place-based-model` | Co-benefit appraisals often report aggregate gains while leaving the causal, geographical, delivery and valuation assumptions behind those estimates difficult to inspect.
