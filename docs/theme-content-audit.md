# Theme and content audit

Date: 2026-08-14  
Status: owner review required

## Method

This audit compared the six owner-directed themes with public evidence from Andrew
Sudmant's SFU and University of Edinburgh profiles, publisher and DOI records, and
named project/tool sites. A public record was added only when an authoritative URL
or stable identifier could support it. Dates shown as `1 January` represent records
where only a publication year was available and should be corrected during metadata
refresh. Author strings containing “and collaborators” are deliberately incomplete
and require ORCID/Crossref reconciliation.

## Taxonomy decisions

- Themes overlap: every project and publication has one primary theme and up to
  three secondary themes.
- Geography, governance scale, method, climate domain and sector are separate
  controlled fields. They are not encoded as themes.
- Canonical records appear once. Theme views cross-list the canonical record and
  identify whether the relationship is primary or secondary.
- The retired ID `canadian-comparative-policy` is invalid. The canonical ID is
  `canadian-climate-policy` and the display title is **Canadian climate policy**.
